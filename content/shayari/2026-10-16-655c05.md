Raat ki khamoshi mein, dil ki dhadkan sun ke,
Har saans mein teri yaad, ek doosra makaan milta hai.
Shanti ke andar teri hawa bhi chup chup ke gate,
Ab khamoshi mein bhi teri yaad rehti hai.
