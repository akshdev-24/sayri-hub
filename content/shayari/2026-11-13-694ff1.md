---
title: "Chand ki Khamoshi"
date: 2026-11-13T11:26:02Z
category: "motivation"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "chand"
---

Chand raat ke aandhere mein chup baitha, maine apna ujala khud nikala,
Woh chandni sirf kinaron tak rahi, maine safar apne pairon se chalaya,
Chand ne kuch nahi kaha, phir bhi maine haath nahi jode,
Josh se bhara hai yeh dil, shikayat sirf chand ki khamoshi se.
