Khamoshi ki ore badhte kadam, tumhare saath har raah roshni hai.
Dil ki dhadkan ab nayi ummeed bolti hai, josh se bhare sapne hakeekat ban rahe hain.
Tumhare saath ye safar khubsurat, har manzil ab paas lagti hai.
