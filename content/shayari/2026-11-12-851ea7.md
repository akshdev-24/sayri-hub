---
title: "Utara diya Mujhe"
date: 2026-11-12T11:42:43Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "shikayat bhara"

---

Tune safar chuna, mujhe raste ke kinare utaar diya,
Har gaadi tere naam ki aahat leke nikal jaati hai,
Yahan se kitne chakkar hue, mera intezaar bas adhoora reh gaya,
Kis mod par chhod gaya tu — teri khamoshi se poochho
