Dil ke kone mein ek dard chhupa hai, raat bhar sirf tera naam leta hai
Raat ki khamoshi mein dil ne pukaara, chai ki thandi khushboo ke saath
Ab shor kam hai dil ke kinare par, khamoshi baith gayi, sapno ki uddan dhoondhenge hum
Mere dil ki dhadkan tere intezaar mein, subah ki kiran le aayi naya raag
