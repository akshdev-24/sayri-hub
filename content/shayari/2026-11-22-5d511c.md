Aaina poochhta hai, itni roshni kahan se laate ho,
Main kehta hoon teri aankhon mein jhaank lo, wahin to jalti hai,
Girna aadat nahi meri, sambhalna meri pehchaan hai,
Tu saath chal, toh toote aaine bhi dekh kar muskurayenge
