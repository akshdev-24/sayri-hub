Ek diya jalayi raat ki khamoshi mein, yaadon ki roshni peeta raha hai.
Shore kam hai dil ke kinare mein, moon light basa hue, dil ki dhadkan girne hai.
Manzil ki raat ki chhamni mein baarish ne teri yaad karechi gayi, hawa mein saaf na hai.
