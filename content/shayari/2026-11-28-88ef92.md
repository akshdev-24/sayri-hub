title: "Intezaar ka Diya"

---

Jalaya tha diya, tere intezaar ki raaton mein,
Sukoon mila hai, jaise koi gehri neend baithi ho dil mein.
Raat bhi ruki hai, hawa bhi thami si hai,
Bas tu aur ye chandni, meri raah dekhti hai.
