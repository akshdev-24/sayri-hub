---
title: "Safar Ki Dosti"
date: 2026-10-06T10:41:38Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "sukoon wala"

---

Raat ki chhamni mein hat par aaina bina balki khamosh baatein,
Tum saamne ho to aaina bhi khamosh rehta hai, duniya bhi manzoo karti bane;
Baarish ki dhun mein safar hai zindagi, dil ko chod ke puri manzil.
