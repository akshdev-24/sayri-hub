Raat ki intezaar mein, dil ki dhadkan mere tanhaiyon ke saaz
Subah ki pehli hawa mein chai bhi adhoori, zindagi udaas, har pal teree yaad ka intezar
Tanhaiyon ka jhoka, dushmani se bhara, main khamoshi se guzarta hoon
