Barish ki boondein teri yaadon ke saath, dil ko naya motivation ka dhuan deti hain.
Har ek baarish ki baarish mein, sapno ki roshni phool kar ujwal ban jati hai.
Motivation ki har boondein, teri hawa mein basa, zindagi ka safar ban jata aadhuri.
