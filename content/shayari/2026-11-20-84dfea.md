---
title: "Khaali Aangan"
date: 2026-11-20T12:02:14Z
category: "yaadein"
mood: "udaas"
keyword: "tanhai"
---

Tanhai ke is aangan mein, teri yaad ki dhoop utarti hai,
Har aahat pe dil samajhta hai, tu laut ke aati hai.
Khamoshi se lipat ke, din guzar rahe hain mere,
Tere bina ye raaste, khud se hi baat karte hain.
