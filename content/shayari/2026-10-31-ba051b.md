---
title: "Hawa mein Dosti"
date: 2026-10-31T09:54:40Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "udaas"
keyword: "hawa"
---

Hawa mein chai ki khushboo hai, dil ki saans se banta hai,
Raat ki tanhai mein tere sapne, ek nasa dosti ka sang.
Khamoshi mein tera naam khoya aaya, dil ki hawa mein ruchha,
Barish ka aap saath, zinda hai meri dosto ki yaad.
