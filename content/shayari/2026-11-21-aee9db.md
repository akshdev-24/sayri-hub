Baarish ki boondein teri yaad ki, dil ki dhadkan abhi bhi intezaar mein
Har boondein se baat hai, josh mein bhare, tujhko milne ka intezaar
Aasmaan bhi rishte se bhar aaya, par raah abhi bhi tere intezaar ki
