---
title: "Jhutha Aaina"
date: 2026-11-28T12:11:47Z
category: "yaadein"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "aaina"
---

Aaina jhuth bolta hai, kehta hai tu laut aayega,
Meri har shikayat ko teri yaad mein badal jaata hai.
Main andar se toota hoon, par ismein meri galti kya,
Tu hi bata, aaina kyun tera chehra dikhaata hai?
