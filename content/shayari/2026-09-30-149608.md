Yaadein hain fuel, dil hai engine, chal pade hain bekhauf,
Manzil door sahi, par hausle ka hai yehisliyaq.
Raste puchte hain thakaan ka pata, hum muskurake talte hain,
Yaad teri aakey, har mod ko manzil bana deti hai.
