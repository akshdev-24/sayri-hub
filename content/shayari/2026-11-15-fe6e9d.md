---
title: "Yaar Ka Aaina"
date: 2026-11-15T11:24:18Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "josh wala"
keyword: "aaina"
---

Aaine mein chehra nahi, tere jaisa yaar dikhta hai,
Saath ho tu to har musibat ka hisaab barabar dikhta hai.
Dosti ka yeh josh hai, na kam hoga na thanda,
Tere bina yeh aaina bhi mujhe be-mayaar dikhta hai.
