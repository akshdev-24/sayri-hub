Manzil pe pohochne ki ummeed thi, lekin raaste mein khud hi rasta bhul gaya
Har mod pe tere yaadon ka saaya, lekin manzil abhi bhi door nazar aati hai
Shikayat hai ke safar adhura, manzil bin adhuri si lagti hai
