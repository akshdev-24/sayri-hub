Raat ki khamoshi mein bhi shor uthta hai mera,
Yaadon ne aag lagayi hai, ab dhuaan nahi uthta!
Tere bina yeh sannata bhi cheekh kar bolta hai,
Josh-e-dil hai itna, ke khamoshi bhi jal uthti hai.
