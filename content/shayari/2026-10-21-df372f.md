Raat ka andhera sajaya, phir bhi dil mein chingaari
Sapno ke paar kadam badhaya, kuch toh raasta hai bhari
Raat bhar jagne ka hosla, dil ki dhadkan se hai sara
Zindagi se ek raat bhar, phir bhi josh se karna ishara
