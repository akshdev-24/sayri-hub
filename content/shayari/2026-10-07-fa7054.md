Raat ki shaam mein tu aayi, meri har saans mein sukoon ki behn.
Zindagi ke safar mein teri yaadein bhi woh raat jaisi, jahan andhera bhi roshni se bhare.
Dil ki dhadkan ab bas ek hi ghamand se guzarti hai, jab tere khoj mein milti hai raat ki hawa.
