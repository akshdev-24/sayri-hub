Dil ki dhadkanon se jhinkar, josh ban ke chale hain hum,
Raat ki hawa mein teri yaad, har saans mein baje phoolon ki khushboo hai.
Har sapne ko chhoo lete hain, jab dil hai full of fire,
Safar hai zindagi, tu chhod, phir bhi dil mein hai tera shor.
