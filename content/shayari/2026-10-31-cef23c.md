---

Chand, tum toh bas nazar mein khada ho, meri sunwai karo yahi khwahish hai
Intezar ka dard, raat bhar tumhari roshni mein chup ke rehta hai
Magar tum, chand, kya samjhe yeh bechaini?
Tumhari chhaya mein, meri khwahish khamosh rehti hai
