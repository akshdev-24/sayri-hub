Maine khud ko diya tha ek sapna, lekal ab raat mein uski khamoshi mujhe rok deti hai
Har sapne ki aag bujh rahi hai, par mann ke andar ek diya jal raha hai ummeed ki
Chal, ye diya thaam le, phir se safar chal, kyonki shikayat ke bheege pal bhi rastaya dete hai
