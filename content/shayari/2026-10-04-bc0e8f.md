Aaina mein dekha apna tukra tukra sapna, lekin har tukko mein josh ki chingari
Dil ka dard thoda siyaahi, par saans mein hai aag ki awaz
Har shaam se uthti hai ummeed ki dhun, aaina bhi muskurane ki koshish karta
