Main safar se laut aaya, lekin tere bina raasta adhoora lagta hai
Har mod pe yaadon ki chhaon, par teri absence ka saans utharta hai
Tere bina safar ka maza fade ho jaata, dil ki dhadkan thi hamari manzil
Ab har safar ka milestones teri yaad se bhari, par manzil tune chhodi nahi
