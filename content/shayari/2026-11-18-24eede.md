Chand ki roshni mein meri raahon ka intezar, dil khush ho jaye sukoon se
Har lamha teri yaad ka rang le aayi, jaise chai ki khushboo raat bhar
Tere bina chand bhi adhoora, par teri hawain se zindagi mein sukoon milta hai
