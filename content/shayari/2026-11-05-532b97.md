Subah ki thandi hawa mein, dhadkanon ki dhun suno, zindagi ki shanti milti hai tujhse.
Subah ki pehli kiran, jaise chai ki garamiyon ki meethi meethi khushboo, dil ko sukoon deti hai.
Har subah ki raahon mein, yaadon ki roshni se, khamoshi ban ke jeevan ki saans ban jati hai.
