title: "khamoshi ka intezaar"

---

Khamoshi mein bhi tera intezaar kaanp raha hai,
main chup hoon, par dil koi baat kehta ja raha hai.
Raat guzar gayi, subah aa gayi, tu nahi aaya —
ab meri saans bhi tere naam se ruk jaati hai.
