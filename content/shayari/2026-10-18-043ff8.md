title: "Safar ki Shikayat"

---

Safar ka har mod pe, dil keh raha hai, 'Thoda ruk, thoda dekh.'
Le shaq ki gehrai se, ummid ki dhadkan nahi rukti, chalte chale.
Kyunki mann ki shikayat bhi rasta banati hai, mann ko sambhal ke.
