date: 2026-10-12T11:16:01Z

---

Tere is safar pe rootha hai mera har lamha,
Jo milne se pehle hi tanha kar jaata hai.
Shikayat hai teri, ki saath rehna tha waasta,
Intezaar ki is dhund mein main bhaatak chuka hoon.
