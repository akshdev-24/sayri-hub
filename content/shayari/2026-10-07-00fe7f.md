Har sapne ko hawa ka ek doosra rang milta hai, soch se bhi behte laage hai.
Manzil teri nazar ke pehle hi pahunche gayi hai, hawa bhi teri shaqs se baandh rahi hai.
Dil teri haar se kuchh ho jaata hai to, haal ki hawa bhi roshan huve hai, josh se bhare hue hai.
