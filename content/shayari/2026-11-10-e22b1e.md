Raat ki khamoshi mein dil ke khwab sunne mein chai ki maaz hi zindagi khatma hota hai.
Subah ki hawa mein ek song milta hai jo tum se pasand hoti hai.
Barish ki thandi hava mein tum kharo ro ho, zindagi ke raahon mein yaadon ka safar chalu hai!
