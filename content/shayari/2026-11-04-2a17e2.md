Raat ki khamoshi ke saath teri yaad aati hai, chai ke safar bina dhundne ki khamoshi mujhe rokti hai
Dil ki dhadkan baarish ki thandi hawa mein paida hui hai, raat bhar uski pehchaan kar diwana
Zindagi ke raahon mein aaina mein balki khamosh baatein reh sakte hain, dil ko na apne hisaab se chalne dhan
Subah ki pehli kiran teri yaad lekar aayi, but raat bhar jaisi khamoshi ne usko baaki kiya
