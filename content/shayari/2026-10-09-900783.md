Raat ne chhua andhera, lekin teri yaadon ne diya kitaara chandni
Har khamoshi mein teri adhura si baat, dil ki dhadkanon se guzari jati hai
Subah ki hawa le aayi teri yaad, josh se bhar diya meri saanson ki dhun
