Teri yaadon pe ek chai banayi jaaye
Raat ke andhere mein teri muskan ki garam chai
Har sip pe tujhko yaad karta hoon, yaadon ki mehfil e nazaqat
Tere bina chai bhi ek aadat ho gayi hain
