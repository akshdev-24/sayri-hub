---
title: "Dil Ki Dard"
date: 2026-10-08T10:52:25Z
category: "yaadein"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "dil"
---

Raat ki chhamni mein, dil ki dhadkan sun ke, har khamoshi ki baat tumse keh doon,
Subah ki hawa mein tu hi hai, lekin yaadon ka geet bas ek siyah dhadkan,
Tere bina zindagi ki rang phaansi, dhuan‑dhuan reh gaya.
