---
title: "Safar Ki Hawa"
date: 2026-10-03T09:22:46Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "romantic"

---

Rain-kissed silence, I carry your echoes on the breeze,
This heart-vehicle called 'safar' burns with your unspoken prayers.
Intezaar ki dhun mein, every sunset whispers your name—
Chhod ke bhool jaye zindagi? Nahi, tu hi hai meri hawa.
